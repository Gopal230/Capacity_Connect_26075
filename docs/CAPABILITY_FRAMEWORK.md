# CAPACITY CONNECT — Capability Framework & Architectural Principles

## 1. Enterprise Paradigm: Beyond Traditional LMS

Standard Learning Management Systems (LMS) primarily manage course enrollment, content consumption, quiz scores, and generic certificates of completion. 

**CAPACITY CONNECT** introduces an operational readiness layer engineered specifically for mission-critical meteorological and scientific operations:

1. **Role-Based Competency Requirement Mapping**: Direct association of job roles (e.g., Nowcaster, Forecaster, Radar Specialist) with mandatory competency domains and mastery levels.
2. **Gap-First Training Personalization**: Learners initiate paths based on verified assessment of existing competencies against operational benchmarks.
3. **Explainable Trainer & Course Matching**: Multi-criteria weighted algorithm balancing competency relevance, trainer credentials, rating, and workload.
4. **Operational Scenario Lab**: Simulation-based scenario testing under realistic pressure and operational conditions.
5. **Trainer-Verified Operational Evidence**: Independent sign-off by accredited trainers on real-world operational artifacts (analyses, forecasts, datasets).
6. **Operational Readiness Index (ORI)**: Transparent multi-signal readiness metric reflecting true frontline preparedness.
7. **Verified Capability Passport**: Portable, tamper-evident record of proven capability with recertification lifecycles.
8. **Knowledge Continuity Vault**: Systematic preservation of tacit expert know-how with mission-criticality and successor-risk governance.

---

## 2. Multi-Gated Competency Verification Model

Final operational capability verification is enforced through three independent gates:

- **Gate 1: Curriculum Completion** — 100% completion of structured modules, lessons, and learning media.
- **Gate 2: Objective Knowledge Validation** — Successful completion of post-training knowledge assessments.
- **Gate 3: Practical Operational Evidence** — Trainer review, scoring, and sign-off on real-world operational deliverables.

This structure firmly separates **knowledge acquisition** from **applied operational competence**.

---

## 3. Explainable Recommendation Engine

The portal employs a deterministic, transparent matching algorithm rather than opaque black-box scoring. Every recommendation exposes its exact weighted breakdown:

- Competency alignment
- Learning level suitability
- Trainer domain expertise
- Administrative verification trust
- Historical trainer rating
- Trainer schedule and active batch load

This provides full audibility required by public sector and enterprise governance standards.

---

## 4. Knowledge Continuity & Succession Protection

The Knowledge Continuity Vault safeguards mission-critical institutional memory:

- Captures expert debriefs, operational playbooks, case archives, and recorded walkthroughs.
- Categorizes assets by operational criticality (Standard, High, Mission-Critical) and successor risk.
- Ensures seamless transition and continuous capability despite retirements and role rotations.

---

## 5. Decision Support & Organizational Readiness

The **Admin Readiness Command Center** aggregates operational readiness into strategic insights:
- Departmental Operational Readiness Index (ORI)
- Early detection of high-risk personnel and teams
- Evidence verification turnaround rates
- Department × Competency heatmaps for workforce planning
