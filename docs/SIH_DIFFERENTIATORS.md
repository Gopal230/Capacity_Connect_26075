# SIH Differentiation Notes

## Judge question: “How is this different from Moodle or a normal LMS?”

A normal LMS primarily manages content, enrollment, completion and quizzes. CAPACITY CONNECT adds an organizational capability layer:

1. role-based competency requirement mapping
2. gap-first training recommendation
3. explainable trainer/course matching
4. operational scenario testing
5. trainer-reviewed work evidence
6. Operational Readiness Index
7. Capability Passport with credential validity
8. Knowledge Continuity Vault with successor-risk tagging

## Judge question: “How do you prove competency?”

Final verification is gated by three independent checks:

- course learning completion
- post-training assessment pass
- verified operational evidence

Scenario performance is separately tracked in readiness analytics. This separates **knowledge acquisition** from **applied capability**.

## Judge question: “Where is AI/intelligence?”

The prototype deliberately uses an explainable rule-based recommendation engine suitable for government auditability. Each recommendation exposes its score contribution. A production version can later replace individual scoring components with ML models without changing the governance workflow.

## Judge question: “What happens when experienced experts leave?”

Knowledge Continuity Vault captures high-value tacit know-how as expert debriefs, playbooks and case archives, tagged by mission criticality and successor risk.

## Judge question: “What can management do with it?”

Admin Readiness Command converts learning activity into decision metrics: personnel ORI, high-risk capability gaps, verified evidence rate, learning gain, and department × competency heatmaps.
